Mingxin Yao (姚明鑫)

I am currently a lecturer at the School of Business English, Sichuan International Studies University. I hold a Ph.D. in Linguistics from Nanjing University.

My research lies at computational linguistics, corpus linguistics, and discourse studies.

Research interests

  1. Computational linguistics & corpus linguistics
  2. Corpus-driven / corpus-based studies of academic discourse
  3. Business discourse and interactions
  4. Media and social media discourse
  5. Language intelligence & NLP for business language service

Since 2022, I have focused on corpus-driven or corpus-based studies of academic and media discourse. My work has appeared in journals such as Scientometrics, Written Communication, Digital Scholarship in the Humanities, Journal of Aging Studies, Ethics & Behavior, Corpora，Learned Publishing, and Foreign Language Teaching (China). Over the years, I serviced as a peer reviewer for the following journal indlucing Applied Linguistics, Scientometrics, International Journal of Applied Linguistics, Cognition & Emotion, Humanities and Scocial Sciences Communications, Linguistics Vanguard, Discover Computing, and Written Communication,.etc.

For publications, see the Publications page.
