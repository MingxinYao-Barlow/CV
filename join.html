加入我们（中文）

四川外国语大学商务英语学院语言智能实验室 / 重庆市数智商务语言服务工程技术研究中心（筹）负责人 Mingxin Yao 团队现招收优秀本科生及硕士生，欢迎对语言模型、计算语言学、语料库语言学、话语研究等方向感兴趣的同学加入。研究方向的详细介绍请见 LI Lab 页面。

我们希望你

  • 具有语言学、计算机、人工智能、外语、中文等相关学科背景，本科或硕士阶段均可
  • 具备一定的编程能力与相关科研经验；有论文发表、投稿或课程论文经历者更佳
  • 对学术研究有热情，自驱、能独立思考、乐于合作

申请方式

请将以下材料发送至 yao_mingxin@sisu.edu.cn：

  • 详细 CV 与本科及硕士阶段成绩单
  • 简短个人陈述：背景、科研兴趣、研究初步设想
  • 科研写作样本（课程论文、技术报告、投稿论文等，如有请附上）

正式确定合作意向前，通常需要进行一段时间的合作研究，以便双方深入了解、双向选择；课题可基于团队现有方向，也可围绕自拟设想展开。

由于时间与精力有限，如一周内未收到回复，则可视为研究方向暂不匹配，敬请理解。

Join us! (English)

The LI Lab, led by Mingxin Yao (School of Business English, Sichuan International Studies University), is recruiting excellent undergraduate and master's students. We welcome applicants interested in language models, computational linguistics, corpus linguistics, and discourse studies. See the LI Lab page for our research directions. Note: we do not currently recruit PhD students.

Who we are looking for

  • A background in linguistics, computer science, artificial intelligence, foreign languages, Chinese, or a related field; Bachelor's or Master's level
  • Some programming ability and research experience; publications, submissions, or course papers are a plus
  • Passion for research, self-motivation, independent thinking, and a collaborative spirit

How to apply

Please send the following to yao_mingxin@sisu.edu.cn:

  • A detailed CV and transcripts (undergraduate and, if applicable, Master's)
  • A short personal statement: your background, research interests, and preliminary ideas for research
  • One or more samples of previous research writing (course papers, technical reports, submitted papers, etc.), if available

Before a formal decision, selected applicants will typically work with us on a collaborative project for a period of time, so that both sides can get to know each other and make an informed choice.

Given limited time, if you do not hear back within a week, it may mean our research interests do not match at the moment — thank you for your understanding.
