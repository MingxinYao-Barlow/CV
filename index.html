Mingxin YaoPh.D.

Ph.D. in Linguistics · Nanjing University

My research lies at computational linguistics, corpus linguistics, and discourse studies.

Since 2022, I have focused on corpus-driven or corpus-based studies of academic and media discourse. My work has appeared in journals such as Scientometrics, Written Communication, Digital Scholarship in the Humanities, Journal of Aging Studies, Ethics & Behavior, Corpora, and Foreign Language Teaching (China).
